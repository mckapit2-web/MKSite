I am a fourth year engineering student at NC State passionate about research into environmental physiology and variability studies. I have been involved in a range of projects, all leading to my pursuit of studying diving medicine with the Divers Alert Network (DAN).

My current involvement with DAN consists of work on their venous gas emboli variability project, focusing on the correlation between various stress and inflammation factors contributing to decompression stress and how they differ within and between individual divers pre- and post-dive. I am interested in continuing to pursue similar projects in dive medicine in graduate school and in my career.

Originally from Richmond, Virginia, I study and have engaged in research in Raleigh, North Carolina. I have also interned at the Bulgarian Academy of Science, studied in Heidelberg, Germany and am now on exchange in Nagoya, Japan for a year. My hobbies include appreciating and making art, watching live music, riding my motorcycle, and scuba diving.
